Contact

Get in touch with the MockForge team

MockForge is an open-source project first. The fastest way to get help or shape the product is through our public channels below. For anything sensitive, use email.

Bugs and feature requests

Open an issue on the public tracker. Include your MockForge version, the protocol you are mocking, and a minimal spec or config that reproduces the problem.

github.com/SaaSy-Solutions/mockforge/issues

Support

For help with your MockForge Cloud account, billing, or a hosted mock, email support@mockforge.dev. For open-source usage questions, the public issue tracker above is usually fastest.

Security vulnerabilities

Do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities. Email security@mockforge.dev instead. We will acknowledge your report and work with you on a fix and disclosure.

Company and cloud inquiries

For questions about MockForge Cloud, enterprise plans, or partnerships, email sales@mockforge.dev or reach us on LinkedIn. You can also see pricing for plan details.

Privacy and legal

Data export, erasure, and other privacy requests go to privacy@mockforge.dev. Questions about the Terms of Service or the Data Processing Agreement go to legal@mockforge.dev.
